    About Play Union

    Play Union is a drop-in pretend play and craft center — a place for kids to explore, imagine, create, and most of all play. Meet friends, make new ones, and be part of the community. Play • Learn • Grow

    Our approach

    We believe young children learn through imaginative play and hands-on creativity. Our space invites building, pretend, music, storytelling, and making — with caregivers nearby and community at the center.

    Adults are free. Complimentary coffee, tea, and Wi-Fi help caregivers settle in while kids play.

    Debbie Musnikow, Owner

    As a Somerville resident, Debbie cares deeply about the local community. She feels it’s important for children and their caretakers to have space in the community to create, imagine, and explore — and is excited to bring that space to Union Square.

    Debbie loves working with children of all ages. She taught Hebrew to elementary school children with special needs at Congregation Kehillath Israel; served as Unit Head, then Education Director at Camp Young Judaea Sprout Lake; and advised teenagers at New England Young Judaea’s after-school programs. She gained retail and operations experience as a Store Manager and Management Trainer for Discovery Channel Stores, and spent a decade as Operations Director at Stellabella Toys.

    In her free time, Debbie enjoys knitting, crafting, and spending time with family and friends.
